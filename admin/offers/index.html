Publish Special Offer

Use an image poster for normal offers. For video offers, upload the video to YouTube and paste its Public or Unlisted link here.

Leave dates empty to keep the offer visible until you hide or delete it. If dates are set, the website automatically shows it only during that period.

Recommended: vertical 4:5 or square poster, high-quality JPG/PNG/WebP.

The website embeds the YouTube video; it does not upload the video file to Supabase.
